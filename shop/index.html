Mix one stick into 8–16 oz of water. Most people take 2 sticks a day, ideally away from food (about 30 minutes before and after). Always follow the directions on the package.

A bag has 30 sticks. At 2 sticks a day that's about 15 days, so the 2-bag pack is one month.

No. It's a light, natural cranapple flavor. It does contain fish (salmon), so skip it if you have a fish allergy.
