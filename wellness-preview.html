FAQs

Questions, answered.

Is IGNYT an injection?
No. IGNYT is a powder you mix into water — no injections, no prescriptions. It’s designed to work with your body’s own metabolic signaling.
What does it taste like?
Natural cranapple flavor, sweetened with Reb A. 15 calories per sachet.
What is ProGo®?
The patented Norwegian salmon protein hydrolysate inside IGNYT — bioactive peptides produced through gentle enzymatic hydrolysis of Atlantic salmon, backed by more than a decade of research and multiple published studies.
Does it replace GLP-1 medications?
No. IGNYT is a dietary supplement, not a medication, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. If you take medication, talk with your healthcare provider.
How soon do people notice a difference?
Published research on the ingredient measured outcomes over 6–8 week periods. Individual results vary — consistency with a daily protocol is what the research reflects.
What about allergies?
IGNYT is derived from Atlantic salmon, so it contains fish. If you have a fish allergy, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, consult your healthcare provider before use.
