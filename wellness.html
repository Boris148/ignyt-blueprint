What They're Designed To Support

Your body’s own signaling — supported, not replaced.

triGLP and IGNYT are positioned around supporting the body’s existing GLP-1, GIP, and GLP-2 pathways — rather than replacing those pathways with synthetic hormones. These pathways are involved in areas such as:
Appetite and satiety signaling
Glucose and insulin-related signaling
Nutrient use and energy metabolism
Gut integrity and nutrient absorption
The Mechanism

Why the 3-pathway story matters.

Pathway 01

GLP-1

Associated with appetite regulation, satiety, and glucose metabolism.

Pathway 02

GIP

Associated with nutrient signaling, insulin response, and energy metabolism.

Pathway 03

GLP-2

Associated with intestinal integrity and nutrient absorption.

ORYGN’s salmon-derived peptides are described as supporting these existing signaling pathways. The strength of evidence differs by pathway — some findings come from human studies, others from laboratory or preclinical research.
