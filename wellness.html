FAQs

Questions, answered.

Are these injections?
No. triGLP is a small-volume liquid drop and IGNYT is a powder you mix into water. There are no injections and no prescriptions — both are designed to work with your body’s own metabolic signaling.
What is ProGo®?
ProGo® is the patented Norwegian salmon protein hydrolysate behind both products — bioactive peptides produced through gentle enzymatic hydrolysis of Atlantic salmon, backed by more than a decade of research, $50M+ in scientific R&D, and multiple published studies.
Can I take triGLP and IGNYT together?
Yes — the Combo protocol is 1 IGNYT stick plus 0.25 mL of triGLP per day. See the protocols above for the full options.
Do they replace GLP-1 medications?
No. These are dietary supplements, not medications, and they are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. They’re positioned around supporting your body’s existing GLP-1, GIP, and GLP-2 pathways — not replacing them with synthetic hormones. If you take medication, talk with your healthcare provider.
How soon do people notice a difference?
Published clinical research on the ingredient measured outcomes over 6–8 week periods. Individual results vary — consistency with a daily protocol is what the research reflects.
What about allergies?
Both products are derived from Atlantic salmon, so they contain fish. If you have a fish allergy, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, consult your healthcare provider before use.
Where are they made?
The ingredient and manufacturing platform is associated with GMP, HACCP, Non-GMO, Kosher, and Halal quality credentials — see the standards section below.
